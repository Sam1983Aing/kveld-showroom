## Measured

Performance at 1725 x 990 at 2x, headless, Sam's Mac: 60 fps in all 11 parts, p95 17 ms, worst frame 33 ms, no frame over 50 ms (before: two stalls of 433 and 517 ms). GPU busy averaged 42% over the run (39% before, the View guitar sway accounts for some). The bench was never measured: 60% while it scrolls. Hear them at rest 15%.

Sound, the page's real output taken off its bus. No clipping anywhere.
