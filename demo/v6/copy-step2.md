# V6 step 2 · Close-up copy, before and after

For Sam to read. Nothing here is in the page yet.

**What changes:** 17 of the 49 stops. They are the ones that read out a figure the drawing has just shown: scale, fret count, radius, nut width, body depth. It is mostly the Nut and Neck stops, and the Back on a few.

**What stays:** the other 32 stops, word for word.

**The word in *italics*** is the one that takes the guitar's colour.

**Numbers I left in:** ones the drawing does not show and that tell the story, like the angle of a headstock or the millimetres KVELD changed on a horn. Say so if you want those out too.

**One fix on the way:** Moss's nut line named Fender. Nothing else on the page names a real maker, so it's gone.

## 01 · Floe

### Nut

Before
> **Bone, 1.650 in *across*.**
> The strings cross it 0.282 in apart and open out to 0.425 at the bridge, the spacing on the original sheet.

After
> **Bone, where the strings *begin*.**
> A strip of bone at the end of the board. The strings leave it close together and open out toward the bridge, on the spacing the original sheet gives.

### Neck

Before
> **Twenty-one frets, a *7.25* in radius.**
> One piece of maple, no separate board. The radius on the drawing is rounder than most necks today and fills the hand.

After
> **One piece of *maple*.**
> No separate board: the frets go straight into the neck. It is rounder across than most necks made today, and it fills the hand.

### Back

Before
> **1.75 in of *swamp ash*.**
> Turned over: six ferrules where the strings come through, a four-bolt neck plate, and the eighth-inch edge radius that keeps a fifties slab feeling square.

After
> **Ash, all the way *through*.**
> Turned over: six ferrules where the strings come through, a four-bolt neck plate, and a tight edge that keeps a fifties slab feeling square.

## 03 · Scree

### Nut (text only)

Before
> **Pitched back *13* degrees.**
> The head leans back from the nut as the plans draw it, so the strings press down on 1.695 in of bone.

After
> **Pitched back *13* degrees.**
> The head leans back from the nut as the plans draw it, so the strings bear down hard on the bone.

### Neck

Before
> **Twenty-two frets, *bound*.**
> A rosewood board with cream binding, a 12 in radius and 24.75 in scale. The neck joins the body at the last fret.

After
> **Bound in *cream*.**
> A rosewood board with a cream edge from the nut to the body. The neck is glued in, not bolted, and meets the body at the last fret.

### Back (text only)

Before
> **Bevels kept, horns *blunted*.**
> The edge bevels follow the drawn lines. The horns are rounded off and both waists filled out, so the twin horn reads as ours. 35 mm of mahogany under a slate stain.

After
> **Bevels kept, horns *blunted*.**
> The edge bevels follow the drawn lines. The horns are rounded off and both waists filled out, so the twin horn reads as ours. The thinnest body of the seven, mahogany under a slate stain.

## 04 · Moss

### Nut

Before
> **Bone, 1.650 in *across*.**
> The same Fender-pattern neck as Floe and Heath: 25.5 in scale, a 7.25 in radius, twenty-one frets.

After
> **One neck, three *guitars*.**
> Moss is built on the same neck pattern as Floe and Heath, down to the bone nut. What changes from one to the next is the body it is bolted to.

### Back (text only)

Before
> **The offset, *softened*.**
> The treble waist filled out 7 mm and drawn up toward the bass one, both horns shortened. 1.75 in of alder in olive.

After
> **The offset, *softened*.**
> The treble waist filled out 7 mm and drawn up toward the bass one, both horns shortened. Alder under an olive finish.

## 05 · Fjord

### Nut

Before

After

## 06 · Rowan

### Nut (text only)

Before
> **Bone over *mahogany*.**
> A 1.695 in nut on a mahogany neck, the head leaning back 17 degrees behind it.

After
> **Bone over *mahogany*.**
> The neck is mahogany under the cherry finish. Behind the nut the head leans back 17 degrees, faced in black.

### Neck

Before

> Twenty-two frets inside cream binding, the neck pitched four degrees to clear the carved top.

After
> **Tilted to clear the *carve*.**
> Rosewood inside cream binding. The neck leans back four degrees from the body, so the strings clear the top where it rises.

### Back (text only)

Before
> **Cherry over *mahogany*.**
> Under 3/16 in of maple at the rim, 1.845 in of mahogany, as the side view draws it. The cutaway horn is ours: 9 mm shorter and rounder.

After
> **Cherry over *mahogany*.**
> A maple cap over a deep slab of mahogany, as the side view draws it. The cutaway horn is ours: 9 mm shorter and rounder.

## 07 · Heath

### Nut

Before
> **Bone, 1.650 in *across*.**
> The 1962 neck: 25.5 in scale, a 7.25 in radius, the strings 0.282 in apart at the nut and 0.444 at the bridge.

After
> **The neck of *1962*.**
> Bare maple above the bone, a dark rosewood board below it. Heath takes its neck from the 1962 sheet, string spacing included.

### Neck

Before

> A slab of Indian rosewood glued flat onto a maple neck, the way the 1962 drawing has it, with KVELD's clay dots between the frets.

After
> **Rosewood, glued *flat*.**
> A slab board on a maple neck, as the drawing has it, with KVELD's clay dots between the frets.

### Back (headline only)

Before
> **1.75 in of *alder*, cut for comfort.**
> Turned over: the belly cut, and through the edge the forearm contour from the front. Both leave 5/8 in at the rim, as the drawing does.

After
> **Alder, cut for *comfort*.**
> Turned over: the belly cut, and through the edge the forearm contour from the front. Both leave 5/8 in at the rim, as the drawing does.
